# Volume template

On instantiation, change `type` to `volume`, remove `template_type` and set the actual ID, story ID and canon status. Store the note in `05-plot/` and list it in the spine.

A volume is a packaging unit beside the arc. An arc may span volumes, and a volume may close inside an arc. Every numeric field above is unset on purpose: this method supplies no figure for volume length, chapter count or illustrations. The author fills each one and names the source card or decision behind it.
