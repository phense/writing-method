# Source card template

On instantiation, change `type` to `source-card`, remove `template_type`, and replace this heading with the title of the source. Set `id` to the source ID used in citations, `evidence_kind` to what the source is (for example a craft essay, an interview, a publisher guideline or your own analysis of a work), `checked` to the date you read it and `access` to how much of it you could read. A source card is not story canon; keep it wherever the author's workspace instructions place craft notes.
