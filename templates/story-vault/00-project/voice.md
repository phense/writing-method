# Voice sheet

This sheet decides how the manuscript of this story sounds. `V0` means no accepted voice exists yet. The voice trial produces `V1`; every later revision is an author decision recorded in the change log below and in [[00-project/decisions]]. Drafting needs `viewpoint`, `tense` and `distance` set in the frontmatter above; while one of them is `unset`, run the voice trial first.
