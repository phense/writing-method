# Writing lifecycle selection

Check the [writing mode](writing-mode.md) first. Manuscript text and the
working notes of a story or study vault (world, cast, arcs, canon, reviews) go
to the story family that policy describes; nothing below selects a skill inside
that family. Everything else uses the lifecycles below, including a reply about
a story, a vault's README, and documentation or a tutorial video about a story,
its world or the story method.

Decide scale using [writing depth](writing-depth.md). Use documentation-authoring
for one standalone document and documentation-planning for a coherent set.
Continue existing docs/writing work from its recorded phase. PDF delivery follows
reviewed content through documentation-pdf. Small wording edits use focused editing.

A narrated tutorial video uses tutorial-video-scripting, then presentation-video-production,
then presentation-video-review; a correction to an existing video starts at the phase
that owns it. "Tutorial" names the purpose and "presentation video" the form: a video
composed from designed scenes and synthesized narration. Marketing or social clips,
filmed material and screen recordings are outside this method. A narrated story
video is scripted by the story family and produced and reviewed by the same two
video form skills, as the [writing mode](writing-mode.md) policy states.

Shared playbook-authoring and playbook-review support scenario-bound operations
including publication and export recovery; ordinary text or a simple PDF does not
require a playbook. The writing owner records need, review, required rehearsal and
acceptance in the same work. No engineering lifecycle is required to write text.
System behavior changes belong to the optional engineering-method companion.
