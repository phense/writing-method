# Story worldbuilding

Mode check first, under [writing mode](../../shared/policies/writing-mode.md): find the nearest ancestor of the target file that holds 00-project/brief.md and 00-project/voice.md. Only kind story permits manuscript drafting; kind study permits study notes only; kind starter routes to story-bootstrap.

World notes are working notes: plain notes that follow neither the voice sheet nor any instructional document structure.

For technically grounded fiction, read [science and institutions](references/science-and-institutions.md) when capability limits, scientific claims or institutional consequences affect the plot.

Read [rules](../../shared/story/rules.md) and the selected vault's brief, accepted world notes, timeline and relevant plot dependencies. Consult the [source index](../../shared/story/sources.md) only for the needed craft question; craft notes are not story facts.

Work through applicable categories: physical setting and ecology; magic/technology; species and peoples; institutions and factions; beliefs, language and daily life; history; necessary pre-story interactions; distinctive mechanisms. Record irrelevant or deliberately open categories rather than inventing filler.

For a distinctive feature trace its mechanism, limits, access, costs and consequences through ordinary life and a concrete character choice. In fantasy, consider magic's effect on labor, authority and conflict. In science fiction, consider energy, habitats, transit, communication, maintenance and biological constraints. Mark speculative departures and research real claims separately.

Separate biology from culture, citizenship, faction and individual personality. Show variation and disagreement. For contact between groups, identify what each needs, fears, knows and misreads; do not reduce a species to a moral alignment.

Write history as event → participants → outcomes → persistent consequences. Store chronological position separately from reveal order. Label competing in-world accounts and narrator beliefs. For pre-story interactions, show why the opening situation exists and which characters know each cause.

Create or extend readable notes using the relevant [vault templates](../../templates/story-vault/90-templates/world-entry.md). Link dependencies and record a proposed canon change. Before changing accepted history, inspect downstream character knowledge, locations and promised revelations. Keep uncertain details provisional; escalate a published retcon as an author decision.

The work is done for this request when the world slice supports its story purpose, has no unresolved contradiction with cited accepted notes, and names remaining gaps. It does not mean the whole world is finished, and new material remains proposed until the author accepts it. `verification-before-completion` applies only to checks that have a command, such as link checks or template integrity.

Status: experimental. This skill has not been piloted on a story and makes no claim about reader effect.
