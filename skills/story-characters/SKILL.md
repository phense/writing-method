# Story characters

Mode check first, under [writing mode](../../shared/policies/writing-mode.md): find the nearest ancestor of the target file that holds 00-project/brief.md and 00-project/voice.md. Only kind story permits manuscript drafting; kind study permits study notes only; kind starter routes to story-bootstrap.

No run state: this skill has no recovery preamble and hands off to no backlog or run-tracking skill. A story or study vault keeps no backlog and no run directory; its state lives in 00-project/development.md and 00-project/decisions.md.

Character notes are working notes: plain notes that follow neither the voice sheet nor any instructional document structure.

Read the story brief, voice sheet, relevant world constraints and accepted cast notes. Use the [character template](../../templates/story-vault/90-templates/character.md) and [working rules](../../shared/story/rules.md).

For each important character connect a concrete want to a motive, competing value, fear, capacity and limitation. Show a difficult choice that could expose those tensions. A contradiction can make a person interesting; unexplained behavior that contradicts established motivation needs development.

Record observable behavior, physical traits only where useful, expertise, blind spots, secrets, social ties and starting knowledge. Differentiate characters through what they notice, choose, conceal and say, not only appearance or catchphrases. Do not make every character use the narrator's metaphors.

## Voice card

How a character sounds is recorded in the voice card section of the character note, not scattered through the profile. For every character who speaks or narrates, fill the card as described in [voice and tone](../../shared/story/voice-and-tone.md):

- register: how this person speaks to whom, and what changes it;
- habitual attention: what they notice first and what they overlook;
- expertise vocabulary: the terms their work or history gives them, and where those terms stop;
- limits: what they would not say, cannot name or get wrong;
- tic cap: an author-set field for how often a verbal habit may recur. Leave it unset until the author sets it; this method supplies no number.

Dialogue examples on a card are proposed until they appear in accepted prose. A card describes a mechanism of speech and attention; it is not a phrase bank to be reused. When the narration contract of the voice sheet and a card disagree about a viewpoint character, record the conflict as an open decision instead of settling it silently.

## Cast and relationships

Give supporting characters independent needs and relationships; scale detail to their narrative role. Distinguish antagonist motive from the protagonist's opinion of it. Character change may be growth, corruption, a tested stable conviction or another intentional trajectory.

Map relationships as directional and time-dependent: what A wants from B, what each believes, shared history, power imbalance and the event that could change it. Preserve the difference between a secret being true and another character knowing it.

Propose arc milestones as choices under changed pressure, not a personality label that flips on a chapter number. Check that the world permits their skills, travel, resources and influence. End with cast notes, voice cards, relationship changes and open choices; new material remains proposed until accepted under the story's working agreement. `verification-before-completion` applies only to checks that have a command, such as link checks or template integrity.
