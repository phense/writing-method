# Story canon review

Mode check first, under [writing mode](../../shared/policies/writing-mode.md): find the nearest ancestor of the target file that holds 00-project/brief.md and 00-project/voice.md. Only kind story permits manuscript drafting; kind study permits study notes only; kind starter routes to story-bootstrap.

No run state: this skill has no recovery preamble and hands off to no backlog or run-tracking skill. A story or study vault keeps no backlog and no run directory; its state lives in 00-project/development.md and 00-project/decisions.md.

The [#1 prose rule](../../shared/story/rules.md#1-prose-rule-natural-prose) applies to every note this skill writes: nothing in it reads as machine-written.

The review report is a working note. It never restyles manuscript, and it does not judge voice or tone; that belongs to `story-voice-review`.

Read the relevant accepted notes, manuscript passages and the [review template](../../templates/story-vault/90-templates/review.md); read the [retrieval contract](../../shared/story/retrieval.md) only if a memory or retrieval tool is in use. Establish the exact chapters and revisions in scope. Summaries and retrieval hits locate evidence; original passages decide findings.

Feedback from the author or a beta reader about facts, chronology, knowledge or promised payoffs is handled here. Treat each comment as a claim to check against the vault, not as an instruction to change the text: locate both passages, then classify the result as below. A reader's confusion can be a real finding even when no contradiction exists; record it as a craft concern.

Check time and travel; physical locations and object ownership; injuries, deaths and abilities; resource constraints; relationship state; viewpoint/character knowledge; names/identities; setup and payoff order; and causal prerequisites. A flashback, mistaken belief or unreliable account can explain a mismatch; verify that explanation rather than guessing it.

For every finding cite both the chapter passage and the conflicting rule, state or earlier passage. Classify it as confirmed contradiction, missing evidence, intentional exception supported by text, or craft concern. Mark severity by its effect on comprehension and future dependencies, not by how easy a line is to change.

Before flagging a capability mismatch, compare the categories and speaker authority in both passages: raw output versus a shaped technique, belief versus demonstrated limit, and a reader's earlier inference versus later explanation. Preserve the earlier reader state even when updating the current model. Do not assume a shared conversation produced a shared agreement; track intended and understood meanings separately when the text supports a difference.

Keep public reputation, official records, private belief, stated intention and observed behavior separate. Track a promise by its wording and later conduct; literal compliance may leave its social purpose unmet. After a victory, check surviving costs and obligations before treating the next chapter as a fresh state.

For documents inside the fiction, including epigraphs, news and case files, record both the event date and when the reader encounters the claim. Identify the attributed speaker or institution; a visually authoritative format does not make an allegation objective truth. Later disclosures may corroborate only part of a report.

When minds, bodies, copies or remote avatars diverge, track each instance separately: origin, current embodiment, active consciousness, memories available and who knows the relationship. A stored copy is not automatically an active person; a shared origin is not proof of shared current knowledge. Use this ledger only when identity mechanics matter to the work.

Compare proposed new facts with accepted canon. Resolve duplicates by stable ID. For a proposed retcon list dependent chapters, historical events, character knowledge and future payoffs. Preserve previously published versions until the author chooses the revision strategy.

Recommend the smallest coherent repair. Implement only within the authorized revision scope. After a repair, reread affected passages and dependencies and refresh changed summaries; where a memory tool is used, flag stale retrieval derivatives. Do not silently reinterpret the story to make a report pass.

## Completion and acceptance

Finish with scope, the named revision of every file reviewed, findings and supporting passages, repairs performed, remaining decisions and limits. No conflicts found in three chapters does not prove the whole serial consistent. A new review is required after material revisions.

A chapter is complete when two things exist: a canon review at a named revision, and the author's recorded acceptance of that revision. This skill supplies the first. The second is an author decision, not a command result: record it in the chapter card or short piece, together with the voice sheet revision in force (`voice_revision`), and in the manuscript index, only when the author has given it, and never infer it from a clean report or from silence. `verification-before-completion` applies only to checks that have a command, such as link checks, template integrity or an export. A story vault has no backlog; open review decisions go to `00-project/development.md` and `00-project/decisions.md`.

Status: experimental. This skill has not been piloted on a story and makes no claim about reader effect.
