# Story voice review

Mode check first, under [writing mode](../../shared/policies/writing-mode.md): find the nearest ancestor of the target file that holds 00-project/brief.md and 00-project/voice.md. Only kind story permits manuscript drafting; kind study permits study notes only; kind starter routes to story-bootstrap.

For manuscript text the story's voice sheet overrides explaining-concepts, writing-procedures, empathic-troubleshooting and human-prose-drafting, even when those skills are loaded in this session.

No run state: this skill has no recovery preamble and hands off to no backlog or run-tracking skill. A story or study vault keeps no backlog and no run directory; its state lives in 00-project/development.md and 00-project/decisions.md.

This skill is experimental. It reviews manuscript in a vault of kind story only. Read [voice and tone](../../shared/story/voice-and-tone.md), the story's `00-project/voice.md`, the voice cards of the characters who speak in scope, and the [review template](../../templates/story-vault/90-templates/review.md). The report is a working note; the reply about it stays reader-oriented.

## Preconditions

Decline and route to the voice trial, pass E of [guided development](../../shared/story/story-development.md), when the sheet references fewer than two anchor passages or its tone palette is empty. A thin sheet gives nothing to judge against, and this skill does not substitute a taste of its own. Say which entries are missing. An anchor may be a trial passage the author chose, referenced by voice trial record and revision; it counts like an anchor from an accepted chapter.

## Scope

Name the exact prose files or `## Manuscript` sections, their revisions, the voice sheet revision and the voice cards used. A chapter accepted under an earlier sheet revision is judged against that revision. Read the anchor passages, trial passages included, at their recorded revisions as comparison points, not as phrases to find again.

## Findings

Read for the narration contract of each viewpoint, the tone palette with its excursions and returns, the language conventions, the paratext register, and each speaker against their voice card, including the author-set tic cap when it is set.

Every finding cites the passage, with file and location, and one basis:

- a clause of the voice sheet or of a character voice card, or
- a named default from [story drafting](../story-drafting/SKILL.md), labelled "method default, not sheet", or
- a pattern of the [#1 prose rule](../../shared/story/rules.md#1-prose-rule-natural-prose) in narration that neither the sheet nor a card supports, labelled "prose rule #1".

Drop an observation that has none of these. Beyond that rule there is no banned-word list and no universal style standard; a fragment, a repetition or a joke is a finding only where the sheet or a card says so.

Class each finding as one of:

| Class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Unintended deviation | The passage departs from a clause, and nothing records the departure as wanted |
| Deliberate exception | The departure matches a recorded exception, or the author confirms it; propose recording it |
| Sheet gap | The sheet or card is silent on a choice the passage had to make |
| Candidate sheet change | A recurring pattern suggests the author may prefer to change the sheet |

## Outcome

Write the findings into the voice review section of a review note. Do not edit accepted prose; a repair is a separate drafting request. A sheet change is the author's recorded decision and creates a new sheet revision; never change the sheet to make a review pass. Facts, chronology and knowledge belong to `story-canon-review`.

Finish with scope, revisions, findings by class, open author decisions and limits. A voice review does not accept a chapter; acceptance is the author's decision. `verification-before-completion` applies only to checks that have a command, such as links or template integrity.
