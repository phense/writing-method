# Sequential fiction study

Use when studying an existing novel to develop transferable craft knowledge. Create a separate analysis vault from the [story starter](../../../templates/story-vault/START.md) at the path the author or the workspace instructions give; this method fixes no location. Set `kind: study` in the frontmatter of the vault's `00-project/brief.md` and `00-project/voice.md`. A study vault permits study notes only: never draft manuscript against the studied work's canon. Attribute the author and primary publication. Study vaults are private working material; keep them out of any distributable package.

Initialize each study vault as an independent Git repository and keep it out of any parent repository's index. Where a memory or retrieval tool is used, the vault's Git root is the scope boundary; verify the returned scope after saving. See the [retrieval contract](../../../shared/story/retrieval.md); skip it when no such tool exists.

## Reading and coverage

Inventory the actual table of contents, preserving publication order and side stories. Record URL, title, stable reading order, access date and one of unread, partial, read, analyzed. A retrieved page or a generated summary does not prove full reading. Inspect the chapter through its final narrative paragraph; truncated tool results require further reads. A changing cached page needs a fresh coverage check. Keep author notes, reader comments, advertising and injected platform messages outside the narrative analysis; preserve intentional typography.

Read sequentially. Before advancing, record a concise original chapter critique and its knowledge delta. Update the relevant project sections or record no new evidence. Preserve prior reader states rather than rewriting early uncertainty with hindsight. If older chapter analyses were produced before the projection existed, describe that as backfilled indexing, not a historical snapshot.

Use source links and scene landmarks for evidence. Prefer compact paraphrases; no chapter archives, exhaustive replacement retellings or phrase banks. Scope any retrieval derivatives to the study vault's Git root and include coverage/revision. Keep fictional facts out of global craft guidance.

## Authorized parallel reading

When the user explicitly chooses parallel blocks, give each worker a disjoint publication-order range and isolated outputs. Read sequentially and record deltas within each block. Label these as block-local observations: later workers must not infer unseen setup or pretend to share a continuous first-reader state. Keep the central ledger unchanged until the coordinator checks full-read coverage and integrates records in publication order. Reconcile names, chronology, changing rules and setup/payoff across every block boundary. Record who read which range and distinguish coordinator synthesis from direct rereading. Parallel coverage is not an independent literary evaluation. If access failures force a changed reading order, preserve the actual order and recovery record; sorting the final notes must not erase that limitation.

## Chapter lenses

- Separate events observed, narrator assertions, character reports, beliefs and analyst inference. Record who learns what and when. A later explanation can narrow an earlier rule without proving a contradiction.
- Track the change in goal, obstacle, decision, resource or relationship. Distinguish narrative order from world chronology and delayed backstory.
- Identify viewpoint and distance, attention, sentence/paragraph movement, dialogue versus inner language, and the function of a formulation. Explain effects instead of collecting signature expressions.
- For humor, identify expectation, misdirection, changed interpretation and reaction. For dramatic buildup, identify the prepared vulnerability, trigger, consequence and recovery time. Note when the same detail changes emotional meaning.
- Track setup and payoff separately from open curiosity. A reader question is not automatically a promised resolution. Preserve local closure even when a larger question continues.
- Record social/ethical friction and uncertain reader effects alongside successful techniques. Do not convert every recurring device into advice.

## Skill improvement

Maintain a promotion log: observation -> evidence -> general mechanism -> candidate practice -> exception -> test. Repeated occurrences within one work are not independent evidence of universal effectiveness. Compare a distant later passage or counterexample before calling a pattern stable. Transfer mechanisms, not an identifiable author's phrase patterns or distinctive stylistic bundle.

Test a candidate on a wholly original scene with different characters, premise and diction. State the observable target, such as whether readers can distinguish intended from understood meaning. Structural validation proves skill format only; one self-reviewed exercise does not establish improved literary quality. Keep project observations provisional until tested.

End each work interval with exact analyzed/total coverage, next unread entry, unresolved access gaps and changed files. Never equate a prepared vault or chapter inventory with a completed study.

## Dense concept studies

When the user wants explanatory context for many fictional concepts, keep chapter occurrences separate from distinct research topics. Map each meaningful occurrence to a short explanation or an explicit unresolved/fictional-only disposition. Group related mechanisms without erasing different uses. Explain the concept in ordinary language, connect it to the cited scene, then distinguish a real analogue from the invented extension. Shared names do not establish equivalence: a fictional technical term may exceed or contradict its scientific namesake. A coverage map proves the notes were addressed, not that all fictional technology is feasible or every source was exhaustively reviewed.

## Resuming a very large study

When the user requests checkpoints, persist each completed chapter record before opening the next. Preserve stable source order separately from printed numbering; explicitly decide how interludes count. Keep fully read but not yet integrated worker records discoverable. The checkpoint names coverage, last completed block, exact next unread URL, outstanding access gaps and pending integration. A block-complete flag requires its report and verified coverage; it never means the whole novel is complete. Record the actual parallel reading order. Source retrieval caches are not reading receipts. After a reset, reconcile records and ledger before rereading or skipping anything. Use the user's chosen cadence; do not impose a 25-chapter block on other projects.
